Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

DokScan is a website for scanning paper forms and filling them in on screen. It is run by BlixerMixer, based in Sweden (“we”), who is the data controller responsible for your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This notice explains what information DokScan handles, where it goes and what you can do about it. Questions: Blixxzt13@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhyWhere it is stored
Your email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you inSupabase (our account and database provider), on servers in the EU. Passwords are stored only in encrypted (hashed) form; we can never see them.
Your documents: scanned page images, the text recognised in them and everything you type, tick or changeTo save your work and show it on every device where you sign inSupabase, in your private account on servers in the EU, and a copy in your browser on each device you use
Sign-in session and settings (language, last opened document)To keep you signed in and remember your choicesYour browser’s local storage on that device

Text recognition (OCR) runs entirely in your browser. Images are not sent anywhere to be read.

Legal basis

We process your email address, password and documents because it is necessary to provide the service you sign up for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), performance of a contract). Technical data such as IP addresses in server logs is processed for security and to keep the service running (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests).

Who can see your documents

Only you. Each account can read and change only its own documents; this is enforced by the database itself. We do not look at, sell, rent or share your documents or email address, and we do not use them for advertising. As the operator we have technical access to the database for maintenance, troubleshooting and to act on your requests.

Services involved

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the website. Like any web host it receives your IP address and browser details when you load the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Supabase stores accounts and documents. See Supabase’s privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr delivers language files when you scan in a language other than English. It receives your IP address for that download.
  • GitHub Sponsors: if you choose to donate, the payment is handled by GitHub and its payment processor, not by DokScan.

DokScan has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The only things stored in your browser are those needed for the service to work (your sign-in session, settings and offline copies of your documents), so no cookie consent is required.

Transfers outside the EU

Your account and documents are stored in the EU. GitHub (which hosts the website) and jsDelivr are based in the United States, so your IP address and browser details may be processed there when you load the site or download a language file. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Documents stay in your account until you delete them. Deleting a document removes it from your account and, the next time they sync, from your other devices. Logging out removes your documents from that device’s browser (they stay in your account). Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and everything in it. Email Blixxzt13@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. You can also download any document yourself as a project file, PDF or image at any time. Under the GDPR you also have the right to restrict or object to processing and to data portability. We answer requests within one month.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), imy.se, or to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live.

Children

In Sweden you can create an account yourself from the age of 13. If you are younger than 13 (or the age of digital consent in the country where you live), a parent or guardian must agree before you create an account. Schools using DokScan for pupils are responsible for making sure they are allowed to do so.

Sensitive information

Forms can contain personal details such as health information. Only store what you need, and keep your password private. Use “Log out” on shared computers.
